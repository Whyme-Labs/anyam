# Model costs, quotas, billing, and unit economics

Type: research
Status: open
Blocked by: 22, 25, 27, 29

## Question

What are the measurable cost drivers and abuse surfaces for repositories, storage, operations, search, builds, previews, agents, egress, logs, evidence, and support in every hosting mode? Model realistic workloads, recommend quotas and budget controls, define metering boundaries, and identify viable packaging hypotheses without finalizing public prices prematurely.
