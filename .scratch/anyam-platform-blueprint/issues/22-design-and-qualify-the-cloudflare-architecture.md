# Design and qualify the Cloudflare architecture

Type: research
Status: open
Blocked by: 01, 09, 12, 14, 19, 20, 21

## Question

What Cloudflare-first architecture satisfies Anyam's consistency, isolation, latency, scale, cost, availability, data-residency, and portability requirements? Assign authoritative state and read models across Workers, Durable Objects, D1, R2, Queues, Workflows, Artifacts, Containers/Sandbox, Workers for Platforms, and external runners; identify limits and define mandatory proof spikes and fallback paths.
