# Decide open-source distribution and licensing

Type: research
Status: open
Blocked by: 05, 06

## Question

What open-source, commercial, and protocol-licensing structure best supports hosted SaaS, customer-owned Cloudflare deployment, community trust, contributor adoption, sustainable revenue, and defensibility? Compare realistic licenses and governance constraints, then recommend what is open, what is separately packaged, and how compatibility and export promises are protected.
