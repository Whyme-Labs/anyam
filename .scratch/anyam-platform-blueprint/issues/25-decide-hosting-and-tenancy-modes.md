Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 07, 14, 22, 24

## Question

How should fully hosted SaaS, managed customer-account, and customer-operated Cloudflare editions share code while preserving ownership, isolation, upgrades, supportability, identity independence, and clear responsibility? Decide Realm and Organization tenancy, Cloudflare account topology, application-attached portals, custom domains, preview-origin isolation, control/data planes, and the supported boundary for each mode.
