# Prototype the end-to-end developer experience

Type: prototype
Status: open
Blocked by: 05, 10, 12, 13, 16

## Question

What should a solo developer, team developer, public contributor, maintainer, reviewer, and operations owner experience from creating or importing a Project through editing, preview, review, landing, release, production Promotion, monitoring, and rollback? Produce and test a low-fidelity CLI and web information architecture that preserves progressive ceremony and keeps advanced primitives discoverable without making them top-level clutter.
