# Decide enterprise governance and compliance

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 07, 15, 21, 25, 30

## Question

Which enterprise identity, audit, retention, residency, encryption, policy administration, separation-of-duty, legal hold, support, and compliance capabilities are necessary for the chosen customers, and which are later or integration-only? Decide the control model and roadmap without promising certifications or guarantees before their prerequisites are known.
